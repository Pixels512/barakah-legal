Barakah — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 June 2026

Barakah is built to be private by design. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Barakah has no account system and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything you enter (calculator inputs, tasbih counts, preferences) is stored only on your device.

Location

If you choose to share your location, it is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is not stored beyond the current calculation.

Calculations

All prayer-time, Qibla, Hijri, Zakat, inheritance, and tasbih calculations run entirely on your device. No data leaves your phone to perform them.

Children

Barakah does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
