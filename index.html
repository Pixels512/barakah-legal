Barakah · بركة

Your private daily companion for worship

Barakah brings accurate prayer times, a Qibla compass, the Hijri calendar, a Zakat calculator, tasbih, and more together in one clean, Arabic-first app — with everything computed privately on your device.

Features

  • Prayer times with multiple calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, MWL, Egyptian, ISNA, and more)
  • Qibla direction with a live compass
  • Hijri calendar and date converter
  • Zakat calculator (gold or silver nisab)
  • Tasbih counter with common adhkar
  • Inheritance helper (educational estimate)
