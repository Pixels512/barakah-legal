Frequently asked questions

Which calendar does this converter use?

The Umm al-Qura calendar, the calculated Hijri calendar published in Saudi Arabia. The conversion runs in your browser with the same calculation the Barakah iPhone app uses on your device.

Why can the Hijri date differ by a day from my local announcement?

Islamic months traditionally begin with the sighting of the new crescent moon, and local moon-sighting committees can announce a start one day before or after any calculated calendar, including Umm al-Qura. For dates of worship, follow your local authority; the Barakah app lets you nudge the displayed date by up to two days to match it.

When does Ramadan 1448 AH begin?

According to the Umm al-Qura calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 AH corresponds to 8 February 2027. The actual start of fasting follows the moon sighting announced where you live.

Is anything sent to a server when I convert a date?

No. The conversion is computed entirely in your browser on this static page — no account, no tracking, and the dates you enter are not sent anywhere.
